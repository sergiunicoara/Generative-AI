The client-facing workspace exposes the answer first and its technical trail on demand.

Anatomy of an advisory answer
A maintenance recommendation carries its proof

The UI does not present a free-floating AI claim. It renders the decision alongside the current rule, observed value, open work context, source identifiers, and the query that combined them.

Safety boundaries
Useful advice without pretending to be a control system

The POC makes the intended decision boundary explicit. It is designed to earn review confidence, not to bypass operations governance.

Temporal correctness

Rules change over time

The historical scenario selects R1 at 90 °C before 1 June 2026 and R2 at 85 °C after that date. A recommendation has an effective-date context.

Evidence discipline

Unknown stays unknown

Assets with no sufficient telemetry are surfaced as evidence gaps. The system requests further evidence rather than fabricating maintenance risk.

No maintenance conclusion without evidence
Human control

Advisory, not autonomous

The workspace identifies a review priority. It does not issue a command to equipment, approve work, or mutate source systems.

Layering
Dependency direction and operational responsibility

The serving layer stays thin. RDF materialisation, semantic querying, validation, and answer construction are separated from the API and presentation layer.

api/routes/energy_demo.py
Tenant-gated routes expose the dashboard, questions, answer, RDF export, and validation result. It shapes responses; semantic logic lives below it.
graphrag/domains/energy/demo.py
Deterministic EnergyDemoService: materialises source-backed RDF, selects the applicable bulletin, executes the committed query and assembles evidence-backed advisory responses.
ontology/mappings/energy-assets.r2rml.ttl
Checked-in mapping contract for asset and work-order source records, including energy:status and the asset relationship join.
evals/energy_demo/sparql/maintenance_review.rq
Version-controlled semantic query that retrieves the affected asset, observed temperature, authoritative threshold, bulletin and open work order.
scripts/run_energy_demo_e2e.py
Repeatable proof path: creates the synthetic export, materialises RDF, runs the advisory, exports Turtle and checks the candidate validation result.
tests/unit/test_energy_demo.py
Regression tests cover current and historical answers, tenant isolation, RDF serialisation, SHACL rejection, and source-backed R2RML/SPARQL behaviour.
